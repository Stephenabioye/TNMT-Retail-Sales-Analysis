# TNMT Retail Sales Analysis

## Overview
An end-to-end Excel data analysis project exploring **20,000 sales transactions** across four channels — Online, In-Store, Distributor, and Wholesale — covering January to May 2020. The goal was to uncover revenue patterns, channel performance, product trends, and actionable business recommendations.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (data cleaning, pivot tables, formulas, charts)

## Dataset
- **Rows:** 20,000 orders
- **Period:** January 2020 – May 2020
- **Columns:** Order Number, Sales Date, Sales Channel, Currency, Sales Team Index, Store Index, Product Index, Order Quantity, Unit Price, Unit Cost, Revenue, Profit

## Key Metrics
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | $44.4M |
| Total Profit | $12.7M |
| Profit Margin | 28.57% |
| Total Orders | 20,000 |
| Avg Order Value | ~$2,218 |
| Avg Order Quantity | 5.5 units |

## Key Insights

### 1. Balanced Channel Performance
All four sales channels generate nearly equal revenue — Online leads at $11.2M, followed by In-Store ($11.1M), Distributor ($11.0M), and Wholesale ($11.0M). This suggests no single channel dominates, giving the business flexibility in its go-to-market strategy.

### 2. Uniform Profit Margin Across Channels
All channels maintain an identical profit margin of 28.57%, which indicates a consistent pricing structure across the business. This presents an opportunity to explore differentiated pricing strategies per channel to improve profitability.

### 3. Monthly Revenue Fluctuations
Revenue peaked in May ($9.18M) and January ($9.07M), with dips in February ($8.56M) and April ($8.62M). These troughs suggest seasonal demand patterns or gaps in promotional activity during those months.

### 4. Top Product Concentration
Product 36 leads all products with $1.10M in revenue, followed by Product 32 ($1.06M) and Product 1 ($1.02M). Products ranked 4–10 are closely clustered between $975K and $994K, indicating a relatively flat performance curve beyond the top 3.

### 5. Online Channel Leads Revenue
While all channels are competitive, the Online channel generates the highest revenue ($11.2M) and profit ($3.21M), making it the strongest direct channel for the business.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Invest more in Online and In-Store channels** — these direct channels offer greater revenue and margin control compared to Distributor and Wholesale.

2. **Introduce channel-specific pricing strategies** — the uniform 28.57% margin across all channels suggests an opportunity to adjust pricing based on channel cost structures and customer willingness to pay.

3. **Run targeted promotions in February and April** — revenue dips during these months suggest an opportunity to use promotions, discounts, or campaigns to smooth revenue flow across the year.

4. **Prioritise inventory and marketing for top products** — Products 36, 32, and 1 are clear leaders. Ensuring strong stock availability and visibility for these products can protect and grow revenue.

5. **Investigate underperforming products** — products ranked below the top 10 should be reviewed for profitability and strategic fit, with low performers considered for discontinuation or repositioning.
